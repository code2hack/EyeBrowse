## Interaction reminder

The RG runs its own webpage with the built-in pointer and keyboard. Cursor stays visible; no recenter affordance and no Normal/Reading mode. Head/pad-originated scrolling occurs at the full-screen top/bottom boundary only. Extra outward head movement is discarded, so inward reversal does not pay back invisible travel. Keyboard visibility does not move the full-screen edge to the webpage/keyboard divider. The complete gesture, timing, lifecycle and evidence definitions are in the approved R3 contract, not replaced by this reminder.
