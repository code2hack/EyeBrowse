## 1. Binding design and approval

This is an implementation contract, not a mood board. Workers MUST read it with SPEC.md and cite its revision in UI work and review packets. Missing controls, changed ordering, a second toolbar row, wrong orientation or nonconforming black surfaces are defects even when basic browsing works.

The Owner specified: one top row in the order Close, Back, Forward, Refresh, compact address, tab number, More; the bookmark star at the rear INSIDE the address field; multiple tabs and right/left swipe selection; the four exact overflow items; no lower action dock; full use of the 480 × 640 canvas; black background and light contents. The always-visible bounded cursor, edge scrolling and built-in keyboard remain required.

Numeric dimensions, typography, last-tab/boundary selection behavior and the minimal Settings presets below are **Planner completion defaults pending HUD-G1 review**, not invented statements of an earlier Owner approval. Once that exact revision is approved, Workers cannot adjust them silently. A material revision goes through Planner and renewed approval of the affected design.

Earlier generated mockups are NOT golden references. In particular, an image that omits the star or Forward/Refresh, draws a separate title/address row, uses incorrect dimensions/orientation, contains an incomplete keyboard or shows a second cursor has no authority to redefine the product. No old image's omission is a permitted fallback. This edit does not create another image set.

## 2. Canvas, layout and black surfaces

### H01 — Full portrait canvas

Use a **480-wide × 640-high portrait** reference canvas matching the RG display. Dimension pairs always mean width × height. The app uses all available supported display area; no simulated device bezel, decorative outer margin, forced landscape rotation, or landscape content letterboxed inside the portrait canvas. Use supported immersive app presentation where available. Measure unavoidable OS insets and actual orientation; do not remove OS protections to force geometry.

Reference coordinates below are **rendered canvas pixels, not Android dp**. Translate using measured runtime density/rotation and verify the final result. At the 480 × 640 canonical viewport, the reference bounds apply; a larger or rotated backing buffer is not permission to stretch typography. A device mismatch must be evidenced and reconciled, not hidden by cropping screenshots.

### H02 — One fixed toolbar

Toolbar bounds are `(x=0, y=0, width=480, height=48)`. It remains ONE row for browsing, scrolling, address/field editing, loading, errors and app-owned utility states. Long URLs or titles must never push a button offscreen, hide Forward/Refresh, or create another line. There is no separate app-name/title/time/battery row.

Web content normally occupies `(0,48,480,592)`. With the default 200-pixel built-in keyboard, page content occupies `(0,48,480,392)` and keyboard occupies `(0,440,480,200)`. The keyboard changes page geometry, not the toolbar or full-screen pointer rectangle. Menu overlays do not resize the page.

### H03 — Exact palette

| Surface or mark | Token / rule |
| --- | --- |
| Window, toolbar, page backing, address, menu, list, keyboard/key interiors, settings, empty/loading/error background | Opaque **`#000000`**. |
| Primary readable text and enabled icons | `#F2F2F2` or `#FFFFFF`. |
| Secondary text / inactive but visible controls | `#B3B3B3` / `#8A8A8A`; never disappear into black. |
| Thin control/menu/key outlines | `#606060`, 1 reference pixel; focused/selected outline uses `#F2F2F2`, 2 px. |
| Pointer | Light ring and center dot; unavailable ring `#8A8A8A`; optional thin black contrast halo over media only. |
| Selected/pressed state | Brighter outline/text, not a large light or gray filled panel. |

No translucent/frosted background, gradient, wallpaper, gray card fill, decorative glow, bottom fade mask or content veil is allowed in the app HUD. Black remains black across default, disabled, focus, pressed and error states. Glyph antialiasing is not a background fill.

Native controls and page content both need light-on-black output. An author-light page underneath a black toolbar is NOT conformance. SPEC §9 defines the engine qualification and limitations boundary. Photos, video/canvas imagery and actual camera preview are media exceptions, not an excuse to paint the surrounding page white. Do not globally invert photographs to create a fake dark-theme result.

### H04 — Reference typography and pointer

Use readable sans-serif text: 20 px for toolbar/menu/key labels and ordinary app text; 18 px for secondary/status text; 24 px for utility content headings. Webpage typography belongs to the page/dark-presentation policy, not a browser-written article. Do not spend viewport space on a fake title/hero supplied by EyeBrowse.

Icons are approximately 24 × 24 px inside their full hit targets. Use one circular cursor, with a reference footprint of 16 × 16 px including stroke/antialiasing, a light ring and small center dot. Its full footprint remains inside the measured drawable screen. No duplicate pointer arrow is drawn for menus or keys.

## 3. Non-negotiable toolbar inventory

### H05 — Order and bounding boxes

```text
[ × ][ ← ][ → ][ ↻ ][ compact address text ...  ☆ ][ i/n ][ ⋮ ]
                                                   ↑
                                 the clickable bookmark star is INSIDE the address slot
```

All regions are half-open horizontal intervals with `y=0`, `height=48` on the reference canvas.

| Slot / stable test ID | X interval | Width | Label / meaning |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| `hud.close_tab` | `[0,48)` | 48 | `×` — Close current tab |
| `hud.back` | `[48,96)` | 48 | `←` — Back |
| `hud.forward` | `[96,144)` | 48 | `→` — Forward |
| `hud.refresh` | `[144,192)` | 48 | `↻` — Refresh current tab |
| `hud.address_slot` | `[192,368)` | 176 | Compact address INCLUDING its trailing star |
| `hud.tab_counter` | `[368,432)` | 64 | `i/n`, e.g. `2/4` |
| `hud.more` | `[432,480)` | 48 | `⋮` — More |

Inside `hud.address_slot`:

| Child / test ID | X interval | Width | Behavior |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| `hud.address` | `[192,320)` | 128 | Domain/short URL preview or full scrollable editable draft |
| `hud.bookmark` | `[320,368)` | 48 | Outline `☆` / filled `★`; Add bookmark / Remove bookmark |

There are **seven ordered top-level slots and eight independently testable actionable targets**. The star is not an optional decoration, part of the editable URL string, a clear button or an overflow item. All eight targets are present in the View/semantic tree and bounded onscreen. The address region yields its star hit area to the star; tapping the star must not also open the keyboard. The shortened 128-px address text region may elide a domain or horizontally scroll an editing draft. It must never borrow the star, tab-counter or More hit area. The other six top-level controls retain their defined widths; the width correction is not a proportional shrink of every control.

### H06 — Availability never deletes a required button

| Control | Enabled behavior | Unavailable behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Close | Close original current tab once; cancel its editor/scroll/utility work. | Present; no destructive background action if context already changed. |
| Back / Forward | Navigate the original active tab's own history. | Visible, dimmed and non-actionable when no history exists. |
| Refresh | Reload original current page. It does not become Close or Stop. | Visible, dimmed on an empty/unavailable page. |
| Address | Open built-in address entry with the full location available. | Always usable for local recovery when the app itself is operational. |
| Bookmark star | Toggle bookmark for the current committed page. | Visible outline, disabled for an empty/non-navigable tab. |
| Tab counter | Open the tab list and show correct active index/total. | Never a hardcoded image label; empty-state policy leaves `1/1`. |
| More | Toggle the specified menu. | Local recovery availability must not depend on network success. |

The star remains visible in address-entry and field-entry states. It refers to the displayed page, not an unsubmitted address draft. Saving a bookmark must not submit or replace field text. Filled state appears only according to the persistence/result policy; after failure keep truthful state and a compact black-background error.

Do not add a fake lock/security glyph merely because an image showed one. The defined inventory is sufficient; origin information must remain truthful.

## 4. Tab and menu interactions

### H07 — Tabs

`i/n` is one-based: `2/4` means the second of four open tabs. It updates on add, close and selection. Clicking it opens a black, light-text list in the content region, not a bottom toolbar. Each row identifies its tab by title or short location; selected state is an outline/mark. Selection uses stable tab identity, not a stale visible number.

Completion defaults for review: create at the end and select the new tab; do not wrap at list boundaries; close selects the next remaining position or the previous last position; closing the sole tab leaves a new empty `1/1` tab. Opening a new empty tab starts address entry. At least four live tabs must be exercised; an example total of four is not the allocation cap.

Swipe right selects the next tab; swipe left selects the previous tab. Qualify wearer-facing directions against physical/replayed pad events. One swipe produces one selection; it never generates a page Back/Forward action, vertical scroll, click or form submission. With keyboard open it closes that entry session before selection, preserves already applied values, and cannot transfer a pending key to the new page.

Swipes at the first/last boundary are safe no-ops with no wrap, duplicate effect or mode change. During menu/list/settings/bookmarks/scanner states, swipes do not silently change an underlying tab.

### H08 — More menu

Exactly four first-level rows, spelled and ordered as follows:

| Order | Text | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Add new tab** | Close menu, create/select empty tab, start address entry. |
| 2 | **Bookmarks** | Close menu, show local bookmark list. |
| 3 | **QR scan** | Close menu, request/use camera for local URL scanning. |
| 4 | **Settings** | Close menu, show implemented input settings. |

Reference menu rectangle: `(256,48,224,192)`, four 48-px rows. Fill is `#000000`, light text, thin light/gray outline. The existing toolbar remains in place. No additional top-level item or bottom dock is inferred from prior images.

A tap on the covered webpage outside the open panel dismisses it and is consumed; it cannot click the page behind it. The top toolbar remains independently actionable under H06/H09; tapping More again closes the menu. Android Back dismisses it. Opening the menu closes active text entry without deliberate submission, retires stale pending input and stops page edge scrolling. Closing it does not automatically reopen the keyboard or resume the previous edge episode.

### H09 — Bookmarks, scanner and Settings

Utility surfaces replace/cover the content region below the unchanged toolbar. They have clear content titles and local exit controls but do not introduce another persistent toolbar. Covered pages cannot receive taps or edge ticks. Where a list needs scrolling, edge input belongs to that active list only and stops at its own boundaries.

Bookmarks: empty state, item selection to open in the current tab, explicit per-item removal, persisted results and errors. No folders, import/export or other functionality is implied by this contract.

Scanner: black surrounding UI with actual camera preview as media; visible Cancel; decoded bounded URL preview with **Open** and **Cancel**; permission-denied and camera-error recovery. No navigation on decode alone. Preview Open uses the same safe address policy and original tab/session binding. Exiting releases the camera.

Settings completion default: Pointer sensitivity with Low/Standard/High (`0.75×/1.0×/1.25×`) and Edge-scroll speed with Slow/Standard/Fast (`120/240/360` native viewport px/s). Defaults are Standard. Use native black/light choice rows, persist the values, and show selection truthfully. Changes cancel current edge/input work and apply prospectively; they do not recenter or replay motion. There is no light-theme switch.

Toolbar Close always closes the current tab, not the app or merely the utility panel. Native toolbar Back/Forward/Refresh retain browser meanings when available; activating them retires incompatible utility work first. Android Back and explicit utility Cancel/Done are the non-navigation way to dismiss utility UI. A tab or navigation change cancels an outstanding scan rather than applying its result to a replacement context.

## 5. Built-in keyboard and full-screen edges

### H10 — Keyboard layout

The toolbar never gains a second line when typing. The reference keyboard uses `(0,440,480,200)`; the webpage remains above it in a 392-px-high content viewport. Only the page reflows. The cursor remains over the full 480 × 640 app surface.

Reference keyboard rows: first row `[444,488)`, second `[492,536)`, third `[540,584)`, fourth `[588,636)`. Use 4-px row gaps, 8-px outer horizontal padding and evenly allocated key widths with at most 1-px rounding distribution. Native key hit targets must not overlap; ordinary interior key positions must be reachable without touching a screen edge.

| Row | Required controls |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Q W E R T Y U I O P |
| 2 | A S D F G H J K L |
| 3 | Shift, Z X C V B N M, Backspace |
| 4 | `123`/`ABC`, Space, `.`, `/`, **Enter or Open**, **Done** |

Use lowercase/uppercase labels according to Shift. Number/symbol layer includes digits and common address/form punctuation, including `: / . - _ @ ? & = # % + , ; ! ' " ( )`; the Worker documents the complete real key map in its implementation and test evidence. **Enter/Open and Done are separate, simultaneously reachable actions**, including in address-entry. A generated keyboard missing Done does not pass. Key fills are black with light outlines/text, not gray keycaps.

Address editing shows the full draft by caret-following horizontal scrolling within the address text region; the star stays fixed in its own 48-px trailing slot. Bookmarking uses the committed page, not the draft. Long draft text never displaces the star, tab counter or More.

### H11 — Editing and edge ownership

Field focus and useful context must remain visible when editing begins and when layout settles. The keyboard does not intentionally reload the page, submit on dismissal or erase field content. User edge scrolling is not counteracted by repeated automatic field reveal.

The **full app-screen edge**, including cursor glyph footprint, is the page-scroll trigger while browser/keyboard state is active. Neither the toolbar bottom nor the keyboard top is an edge trigger. The top physical boundary may lie within the toolbar: edge residence still scrolls the active webpage; toolbar controls retain interior hit positions for taps. At the bottom with the keyboard visible, the page scrolls and keys do not move.

Page scroll invalidates pending page-hit snapshots when content moves under them. A local key remains eligible only while its own original key, layer, editor and keyboard session remain current; unrelated page movement alone is not grounds to reinterpret its output.

## 6. State transition table

### H12 — State and invalidation contract

`Browsing`, `Address editing`, `Field editing`, `More`, `Tab list`, `Bookmarks`, `QR scan/preview`, `Settings` and `Local error` are utility/input states of one browser, not browsing modes. Tracking health is orthogonal. Every visible app-owned state draws exactly one bounded cursor.

| From / event | Next state and visible result | Required cancellation / preservation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Launch with no restorable live page | Empty local tab, `1/1`, black page/address prompt, one toolbar. | Fresh acquisition; no automatic scan, pairing or hidden scroll. |
| Address tap | Address editing; same toolbar and fixed star; keyboard visible. | Retire old field intent; keep current page; create complete local draft. |
| Invalid address Open | Stay editing; compact inline error; full draft kept. | No navigation or tab replacement. |
| Valid address Open | Browsing/loading in original tab; keyboard closes. | Retire old document/editor/page-hit/edge authority; fresh readiness afterward. |
| Field activation | Field editing with selected context above keyboard. | New actual-editor binding; no stale grant revival. |
| Shift/layer change | Field/address editing with new key labels. | Cancel incompatible old key snapshots; do not mutate a field by itself. |
| Enter in field | Newline or applicable form action in original editor. | One bounded operation; no unrelated form or automatic retry. |
| Done / Android Back while keyboard open | Browsing; keyboard dismissed. | No deliberate submission; retain completed text; cancel uncommitted keys; no auto reopen. |
| Star tap in browsing or editing | Same input state; outline/filled star updates. | Save/remove original committed page; no keyboard/navigation side effect. |
| More tap | More open; keyboard closed; page remains behind. | Stop page scroll and pending click/key work. |
| Covered-page outside-menu tap / Android Back | Return to browsing. | Consume dismissal; no click-through, keyboard reopen or edge resume. |
| Tab-counter tap | Tab list; current index/total shown. | Dismiss text entry without submission; suspend underlying page input. |
| Right/left swipe in browsing/editing | Next/previous tab, or same tab at boundary. | One tab action; no vertical scroll/click; outgoing pending intent retired. |
| Tab row selection | Browsing for chosen stable tab identity. | Preserve its live state; fresh current layout; no old edge rearm. |
| Add new tab | New empty selected tab and address keyboard. | Existing tabs remain; update `i/n`; fail allocation without sacrificing another tab. |
| Close current tab | Deterministic successor or empty `1/1` tab. | Retire closed tab resources/results, scanner and editing; no late mutation. |
| Back / Forward / Refresh | Original active tab navigation/loading. | Cancel incompatible utilities, input and edge work; update toolbar from the matching result only. |
| Bookmarks selection | Current-tab navigation; list closes. | Bind to original selection; normal address policy; no automatic new tab. |
| QR result | QR preview, not navigation. | Camera released after usable result; confirmation remains original-session-bound. |
| QR Open / Cancel / permission failure | Navigate original tab, return, or recoverable black error. | No stale scan applied after tab change; no camera leak or settings change. |
| Settings selection change | Same Settings page, visible persisted choice/error. | End old input/edge episode; apply prospectively; no recenter. |
| Inward pointer movement from edge | Same UI state; pointer returns; scrolling stops. | No accumulated angular/filter debt or queued motion. |
| Tab/document/layout change or utility entry at an edge | Appropriate new state, cursor still drawn. | Old edge residence retired; require deliberate fresh edge entry. |
| Sensor unavailable / app loses active presentation | Frozen unavailable cursor if surface remains visible. | No further page/list scroll, stale click or pending key execution. |
| Resume / tracking recovery | Current appropriate local UI; fresh pointer tracking. | No automatic old keyboard/edge resurrection. |
| Engine/network error | Truthful black/light status with local controls available. | Distinguish network loss from lost tab runtime; do not replay uncertain effects. |

A callback is authorized by original tab/document/operation generations, not current equality of labels or coordinates. Late results can update their still-live background tab only where safe; they cannot repaint another tab's toolbar or re-enable a dead editing/scroll session.

## 7. Strict design and implementation gates

### HUD-G1 — Exact design approval (OPEN)

Required states for the approval record: browsing with all controls; star outline and filled; Back/Forward disabled but visible; More open with exactly four items; address editing including star and Done; actual field/password editing; tab list and `2/4`; last-tab/empty state; bookmarks empty/populated; QR preview/permission error; Settings; top/bottom edge contact; tracking/error state.

Use a native-size deterministic layout reference or prototype consistent with these bounds, not an enlarged illustration used in place of native-size evidence. The Owner may explicitly approve the text/layout contract and waive new illustrative images. Record the exact revision/hash and state coverage. The current request to edit the specification, and previously generated images, are not silently recorded as HUD-G1 PASS.

#28/#29 qualification/refactor work may proceed within their approved scope. Production HUD work requires this design decision, mapped authorized ticket scope and ordinary Manager mission assignment; no separate Planner-authored ticket plan is required. A specifically assigned review prototype is permitted to satisfy the gate. Do not treat an unapproved preview as completed product work.

Workers must not delete a failing requirement or edit a golden reference to match their implementation. A material design adjustment requires Planner reconciliation and approval of the affected design before acceptance.

Implementation approval requires both functional and HUD checks. It does not claim optical readability, human comfort or battery endurance from screenshot/replay evidence.
