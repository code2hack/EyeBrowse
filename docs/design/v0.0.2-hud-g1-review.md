There are seven top-level row slots and **eight separately actionable targets**. The bookmark star MUST NOT disappear, merge into the address tap target, move to More, or be lost during keyboard/address editing. Long locations cannot wrap/hide buttons. No second title bar, bottom dock, recenter button or Normal/Reading mode toggle.

Screen distribution: browser area `(0,48,480,592)`; with keyboard, page `(0,48,480,392)` and keyboard `(0,440,480,200)`. Menu `(256,48,224,192)`: four rows of 48 px **Add new tab / Bookmarks / QR scan / Settings** in that order.

First-party window, control, menu, key, empty/error/loading backgrounds are opaque `#000000` with light text/icons/outlines; primary page background must be black with readable light content under SPEC §9. Media images/previews retain useful real colors. No gray/translucent HUD cards, ornamental gradients, wallpaper, or white transition flashes. One visible bounded circular pointer over all app-owned surfaces.
